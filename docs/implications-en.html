If the claim holds: mathematics and cryptography

Levent Alpöge’s Remark reports a proof of RH and Dirichlet GRH. This companion asks what would follow if independent checking established the exact statements.

Hypothetical, not verified here. Dirichlet GRH does not stand in for every formulation of GRH. The toy corpus proves none of the implications below.
